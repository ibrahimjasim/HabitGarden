What HabitGarden actually is, for the record

A fully native, offline-first SwiftUI + SwiftData habit tracker. Local email/password accounts (SHA-256 hashed) plus Sign in with Apple; habits with an emoji, a daily target of 1–20, an optional goal length, and an optional daily reminder via UNCalendarNotificationTrigger; a completion toggle with undo; a streak calculator that's forgiving about "haven't done it yet today"; a hand-drawn Canvas garden where each habit is a plant whose height reflects its 7-day completion rate, swaying at 30fps under a sky that shifts with the real time of day; and an Insights screen with a Swift Charts bar chart, headline stats, and four rule-based "smart insight" cards (best time of day, strongest weekday, week-over-week trend, most consistent habit). No network calls anywhere — everything lives in the on-device SQLite store SwiftData manages.
