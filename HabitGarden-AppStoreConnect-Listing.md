## Description
*(4,000 characters max — this is the long text on your product page)*

```
HabitGarden turns your daily habits into a garden that grows with you.

Every habit you track becomes a plant. Stay consistent and it grows taller, sprouts leaves, and blooms — miss a few days and you'll see that too. It's a small, visual way to feel your progress instead of just logging it.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create habits with a custom name, emoji, and a daily target — from "drink water" once a day to "stretch" three times
• Tap to mark a habit done. Multi-target habits track partial progress (2/3, for example) right in the list
• Set an optional daily reminder so a habit never slips your mind
• Give a habit a goal length (like "30 days") and watch a progress bar count you down to it

YOUR GARDEN
Open the Garden view any time to see every habit as a growing plant, swaying gently under a sky that shifts from sunrise to night along with the real time of day. Seven days of consistency: full bloom. A rough week: a smaller sprout, but never nothing — every habit still has a seed showing in the ground.

INSIGHTS THAT ACTUALLY TELL YOU SOMETHING
Beyond streak counts, HabitGarden looks for real patterns in how you build habits: your best time of day, your strongest day of the week, whether this week is trending up or down from last week, and which habit you're most consistent with. A 7-day chart and a per-habit streak breakdown round out the picture.

BUILT AROUND PRIVACY
HabitGarden stores everything — your habits, your streaks, your account — locally on your device. There's no server, no analytics, no third-party tracking. Sign in with Apple or a simple email account, and your data stays exactly where you put it: on your phone.

Start small. Watch it grow.
```

## Age Rating questionnaire

HabitGarden has no user-generated content shared with others, no web browser, no gambling, no mature themes, and doesn't collect location or contacts. When you fill out the age rating questionnaire in App Store Connect, every content-based question (violence, sexual content, horror, gambling, alcohol/drugs, mature/suggestive themes, etc.) should be answered **None**. That should land you at **4+**, the lowest rating tier — sensible for a habit tracker with no content of its own.
